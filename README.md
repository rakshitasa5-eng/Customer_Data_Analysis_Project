📊 Customer Data Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior using Power BI.
The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, customer demographics, product categories, revenue trends, and payment methods to support data-driven business decisions.

📂 Dataset Information

    Source: Customer Shopping Dataset (CSV)

    Total Records: 1000 transactions

    Key Columns:

    Customer ID

    Age

    Gender

    Category

    Quantity

    Price

    Revenue (calculated)

    Payment Method

    Invoice Date

    Shopping Mall

✔ Data is clean (no missing values or duplicates)

📊 KPIs Included

    Total Revenue: 2.37M

    Total Units Sold: 2981

    Total Customers: 1000

    Total Transactions: 1000

    Top Product Category: Clothing

These KPIs provide a quick business overview.

🔍 Key Insights

    Female customers generate higher revenue and product sales.

    Age group 28–47 shows the highest shopping activity.

    Clothing is the top-selling product category.

    Digital payments (Credit & Debit Cards) dominate over cash.

    Middle-aged customers contribute the highest revenue.

💡 Business Recommendations

    Focus marketing campaigns on 28–47 age group.

    Increase inventory for Clothing and Cosmetics.

    Promote digital payment offers (cashback & discounts).

    Use gender-based promotions for category-specific products.

    Personalize offers using slicers for targeted insights.
